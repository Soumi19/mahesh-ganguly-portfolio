About Dr. Mahesh Ganguly

Researcher, policy analyst and published commentator working across climate, energy, sustainability, international affairs and public policy.

Who is Dr. Mahesh Ganguly?

Dr. Mahesh Ganguly is a multidisciplinary researcher, policy analyst and published commentator whose work spans energy transition, climate and environmental policy, sustainability, geopolitics, international affairs and public policy. His academic and professional experience also extends to geospatial analysis, public health and clinical research.

Current academic work

His portfolio records work as a Teaching Assistant and Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay) from 2025 to the present.

Research & policy focus

Key themes include climate policy, energy transition, sustainability, environmental governance, South Asian strategic issues, geopolitics and international affairs.

Interdisciplinary work

His wider research experience includes GIS, remote sensing, public health and clinical research.

Public communication

Alongside academic work, he writes policy commentary and analytical articles for national and international publications.

What does Mahesh Ganguly research?

His work connects academic research with policy-relevant questions, particularly around climate action, sustainable development, energy security, South Asia and the wider Global South.
